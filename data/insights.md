### What the book looks like
The acquisition portfolio relies entirely on a single profitable segment to stay solvent. Total net value across the entire book stands at ₹12.39 crore. The High-Value Core segment generates ₹26.65 crore in total net value, effectively subsidizing widespread losses across all remaining segments.

### Where value is being lost
Rapid qualification timelines enable systematic bonus harvesting that severely erodes margin. The Bonus Opportunists segment generates a total net value of -₹7.46 crore while consuming ₹17.74 crore in bonus paid. These customers exploit short velocity rules, recording an average days to qualify of just 9.8 days.

### What the evidence says about intervention
Acquisition outcomes depend heavily on campaign offer structure rather than channel selection. Campaign C007 produces an average net value of ₹2940.0 per customer. In contrast, Campaign C003 generates an average net value of -₹1749.0 per customer.

### Recommended actions
Shut down loss-making campaign constructs on Monday and stop targeting negative-yielding customer tiers. Pause Campaign C003 immediately and halt acquisition for the Do Not Target tier, which currently contains 66060 customers. Shift budget into high-performing offers like Campaign C007 while lengthening qualification timelines to block opportunistic bonus collection.
